About

Each hexagon is about 36 km². Scores combine NASA FIRMS satellite detections, CAL FIRE and interagency (NIFC WFIGS) incidents, and NWS fire weather alerts. Prescribed burns are shown but never raise alerts. This is a triage view, not an official warning: follow local authorities.
